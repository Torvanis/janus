- **Trial key** — a time-limited Business key for evaluation only.

An **active user** is a person who signed in to a gateway instance within the preceding 30 days. Service and team tokens are not users.

## 4. Expiry and renewal

Subscription keys carry a renewal date. After that date the Software enters a 30-day grace period, then a restricted state in which existing configuration continues to operate but new items (models, upstreams, keys, quotas, rules, teams, schedules, captures) cannot be created until a current key is installed. The Software never stops proxying requests because of key expiry.

Perpetual keys, where sold, permit use of any release published on or before their maintenance date indefinitely.
